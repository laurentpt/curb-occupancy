Curb labeller

Drag across the strips to draw a zone (ends at the curb-cut edges). Click a zone to select it, drag its edges to adjust, Delete removes it, N adds a note, 1–4 change its kind. Wheel scrolls along the street. Strips: near views first (curb-cut markings on the ground in front of the row), then far views from the opposite curb (driveways, garages). Grey bars under a strip: cars parked in that pass. Orange band: not parkable per the current regulations. Dashed yellow: never-occupied gaps between parked cars (hints only).
